# Brief

**Objective.** Draft a memorandum on whether a self-represented litigant in a United States
federal district court may move to compel discovery responses that the opposing party has
answered only with boilerplate objections.

**Jurisdiction.** United States, federal civil procedure.

**Format.** Memorandum, no more than 2,000 words, with a short answer first.

**Constraints.** Every legal proposition carries a citation to a rule, a case or a treatise.
Anything the model cannot verify is marked "unverified" rather than dropped or smoothed over.
Close with the open questions a lawyer would need to resolve.
